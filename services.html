Our Dedicated Care Programs

Every senior’s routine is personal. We don’t apply a one-size-fits-all schedule. Our non-medical care plans are customized strictly to what your parent needs to remain active, clean, and content.

Companion Care

Mental stimulation and emotional support. We keep active minds busy with lively conversations, classic board games, card games, walking around local parks, and shared hobbies that bring genuine joy back to daily living.

  • ✓ Sharing hobbies & storytelling
  • ✓ Accompanied walks in the neighborhood
  • ✓ Mental stimulation and daily check-ins

Meal Prep & Light Housekeeping

A clean home is a safer home. Our caregivers prepare fresh, nutritious meals customized to dietary plans and help keep key living spaces tidy to reduce fall hazards and ensure overall wellness.

  • ✓ Nutritious meal preparation & clean-up
  • ✓ Dusting, light vacuuming, and trash removal
  • ✓ Bedmaking and keeping walkways completely clear

Medication Reminders

Consistency prevents hospitalizations. While we do not administer medications or handle medical nursing tasks, we provide essential timely reminders so pre-set pills from organizer boxes are taken on schedule.

  • ✓ Timed verbal reminders for organized pills
  • ✓ Hydration reminders to accompany medication
  • ✓ Gentle log keeping of routine times

Transportation & Errands

No need to stress over coordinating rides. We safely drive and accompany seniors to regular medical appointments, local physical therapy, grocery store trips, or social visits around Tacoma and Lakewood.

  • ✓ Doctor visits & local therapy clinics
  • ✓ Grocery shopping and prescription pickups
  • ✓ Accompanied outings for peace of mind

Respite for Family Caregivers

Take a breath and recharge. You can't do it all alone. Our respite care covers hours or full days so adult children can rest, handle personal work, attend trips, or tend to their own children with confidence.

  • ✓ Scheduled temporary backup care
  • ✓ Full coverage while you travel or recharge
  • ✓ Easy scheduling transition for your parent

Ensure safety through the dark hours. Our overnight companion is there to assist with safe bathroom transfers, monitor sleep disruptions, prevent late-night falls, and provide calming security when it's dark.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you navigate senior care choices.

What are your costs, and do you accept insurance?

We operate as private-pay. We can work with Long-Term Care Insurance policies (which often reimburse non-medical home care), but please note we do not accept Medicare or Medicaid. Medicare typically does not cover non-medical companion care or housekeeping services.

How do you background check and vet your caregivers?

Every single caregiver on our team undergoes a rigorous, multi-tiered screening process, which includes standard national background checks, finger-print reviews, reference checks, and a comprehensive in-person interview. We only hire individuals we would comfortably trust with our own parents.

Will my parent see the same caregiver, or do they rotate?

We strive to keep the exact same primary caregiver assigned to your parent whenever possible. Consistent relationships make the experience far less stressful for seniors and help caregivers spot subtle changes in physical health or memory.

How quickly can care scheduling start?

Usually within 3 to 5 business days after our initial in-home meeting with Maria. This timeline allows us to make sure we coordinate schedules properly and pair your family with the caregiver best suited to your parent’s specific routine.

What are the absolute limits of your care (non-medical)?

As a non-medical care agency, our caregivers are legally and professionally restricted from performing clinical nursing tasks. We do not provide physical medication administration (such as injections or refilling organizers), wound care, catheter management, or physical physical therapy diagnoses.
