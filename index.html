Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Families Ask

Do you accept Medicare or insurance?

Because we focus purely on non-medical companion care and daily help (rather than clinical skilled nursing), we do not accept standard Medicare. We accept private-pay and most Long-Term Care Insurance policies. We are happy to discuss the paperwork during our free consultation.

What services do you not provide?

We are a strictly non-medical care company. We do not provide clinical nursing services, such as injections, wound care, tube feeding, or medication changes.

How do background checks work?

Every single caregiver passes rigorous background checks, references verification, and non-medical skills screening. We only hire professionals we would trust in our own homes with our own family.

Will my parent always have the same caregiver?

While we cannot guarantee 100% attendance due to occasional emergencies, consistency is our main differentiator. We prioritize matching your parent with the same primary companion to foster a genuine, trusting relationship.

How soon can care services begin?

Often we can begin scheduling caregiver matching immediately after our initial in-home meeting. Typically services can safely start within 3 to 7 business days of our first contact.
