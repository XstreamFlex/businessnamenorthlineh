Meet Maria Reynolds & Northline

When Maria Reynolds watched her own grandfather transition into needing daily help, she quickly noticed how stressful it was to navigate care options. Huge corporate networks kept sending rotating caretakers who didn’t know his routine. The lack of reliable updates left the family constantly guessing.

In 2019, Maria decided to launch Northline Home Care right here in Pierce County. Her vision was simple: a small, tightly-managed private-duty companion agency that prioritizes caretaker consistency and keeps families completely in the loop.

Our Commitment to You

How We Solve the Two Biggest Problems in Home Care

Most agencies assign whoever is free, resulting in a revolving door of strangers. We do things differently. Here is how we ensure stability and peace of mind for you.

Consistent Caregiver Matching

We match your parent with a specific caregiver based on personality, schedule, and preferences. Your loved one builds a real relationship with the same companion, rather than meeting a new face every week.

Detailed Daily Written Notes

After every single visit, our caregiver completes a written summary of activities, meals, and mood. We share these notes directly with you, keeping you fully updated on your parent's well-being even from afar.

A Team Built on Integrity

Who is Entering Your Parent’s Home?

We know it takes an immense amount of trust to let someone into your parent’s home. That's why we don't rush hiring. Every caregiver on our team goes through a multi-step screening process.

Comprehensive Background Checks: Including criminal history, reference checks, and driving record validation.

Compassion Testing: We only hire people we would personally trust with our own aging family members.

Fully Bonded & Insured: Complete professional liability insurance for your peace of mind.

Please note: As a dedicated companion agency, our professionals assist with day-to-day lifestyle support but do not perform medical procedures like injections, clinical health checks, or wound care.
